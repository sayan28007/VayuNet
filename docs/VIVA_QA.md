# VayuNet Viva / Judge Q&A

## What problem does VayuNet solve?

It turns fragmented pollution evidence into a single workflow for detection, prediction, explanation and response.

## Why multimodal evidence?

Different sources capture different aspects of an incident. Fusion provides contextual evidence beyond a single source.

## Why predict a plume?

A hotspot alone is insufficient for response planning. The plume model estimates movement and affected area.

## Why population exposure?

It connects pollution intensity and spread with the people potentially affected.

## Why federated learning?

The prototype demonstrates model aggregation across simulated city nodes without centralizing all local training data.

## What does Gemini do?

It receives backend-grounded results and produces an operational explanation, with tools and data sources recorded.

## What is synthetic data?

Synthetic data supports reproducible demos and local development. It must not be presented as live external monitoring.

## How is source attribution phrased?

VayuNet reports a likely source with evidence/confidence context rather than claiming an exact facility without sufficient evidence.

## What if a cloud service is unavailable?

The application exposes the unavailable state and continues with independent subsystems where possible rather than inventing a successful live result.
